What I Offer

Full-Service Design

A complete, white-glove experience from the first conversation to the final reveal. I handle space reimagining and planning, all material selections, design elements, bespoke unique transformations and have teamed up with the finest contractors in the business who offer a complete full experience. From the infancy stage to the final reveal every detail is thought through and specifically created for YOU! We handle all jobs from full home renovations, custom cabinetry and custom solutions, and additions to smaller design needs like paint selection, wallpaper choices and install, bespoke home entertainment centers, custom bars, functional and fabulous laundry rooms, elevated primary bedrooms, exceptional primary bathroom renovations, en-suite bathroom updates, living room refresh, unique office spaces, custom desks, Murphy beds, hidden spaces with surprise elements as well as reimagining exterior refreshes for doors, roofs, pools, decks, landscaping, paint and everything else you can imagine.
