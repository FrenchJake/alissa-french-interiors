About

The Studio

Hello, I'm Alissa

Alissa French Interiors was born out of a personal passion — after transforming my own home, a friend recommended me as a designer, and the rest followed naturally. What began as a love of creating beautiful spaces became a calling.

I founded the studio with a single belief: that beautiful spaces are not a luxury — they're a foundation for a life well-lived. I bring both creative vision and meticulous execution to every project, with an emphasis on elevated simplicity, quality materials, and spaces that feel authentically yours.

My work spans full-service residential design, thoughtful room redesigns, and curated styling. When I'm not designing, you'll find me sourcing vintage pieces at local markets or spending time with my family in our own carefully crafted home.

Design Philosophy

What I Believe

Less, But Better

Every piece in a room should earn its place. I design with intention, editing until only the essential remains — and the essential is always beautiful.

Your Life, Your Space

The most stunning room is one that reflects its owner. I take time to understand how you live before I ever suggest how you should design.

Timeless Over Trendy

Trends fade. I design spaces that feel current today and classic in twenty years — investing in quality that outlasts the moment.
