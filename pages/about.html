About me & Philosophy

My Story

Hello, I'm Alissa French

Alissa French Interiors was born out of a personal passion — after completely renovating and transforming my own homes, a dear friend of mine recommended me to a neighbor who was looking to renovate the home they'd just purchased. I remember saying to her, "I don't have a company." Her response was, "Well, make one." Decades prior to that magical moment, I worked at Cosmopolitan Magazine and Vogue, and even back then friends and colleagues would always ask for my design advice. Over 18+ projects later, what began as a passion project and love of creating beautiful spaces became a calling. Oh, and I was hired on the spot that day.

I founded my design firm with a single belief: that beautiful spaces are not a luxury — they're a foundation for a life well-lived. I bring both creative vision, function and fashion to every client, and add meticulous execution to every project. My emphasis is to elevate a space with simplicity, quality, and authenticity. I love to deliver exactly what my clients want, and over-deliver with my own touches and vision.

My work spans from full home renovations, thoughtful room redesigns, primary bathroom upgrades, additions, living room refreshes, exterior and interior color updates, flooring, wall coverings, lighting, roofing, bathroom renovations, hardware updates, ADA accessibility and so much more. When I'm not designing, you'll find me sourcing vintage pieces at local markets and joining my girlfriends at pilates, yoga, and for long walks. My favorite pastime is spending time with my husband, son, daughter and dog in our own carefully curated house we call The French Home. Welcome to our home — I can't wait to help you with yours!

Design Philosophy

What I Believe

Less, But Better

Every piece in a room should earn its place. I design with intention, editing until only the essential remains — and the essential is always beautiful.

Your Life, Your Space

The most stunning room is one that reflects its owner. I take time to understand how you live before I ever suggest how you should design.

Timeless Over Trendy

Trends fade. I design spaces that feel current today and classic in twenty years — investing in quality that outlasts the moment.
